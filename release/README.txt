1.  THE STORY OF FINAL FANTASY IV
----------------------------------------------------------------

The kingdom of Baron rules the world with its fleet of
airships, the Red Wings. Their captain, the dark knight Cecil,
returns from Mysidia with the Water Crystal, taken by force
from defenseless mages on his king's orders. Torn by doubt, he
dares to question those orders. The king strips him of his
command and sends him, with his friend Kain, to deliver a
mysterious ring to the village of Mist...

----------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE STORY OF THE TRANSLATION
----------------------------------------------------------------

This patch is the English build of the French translation
project (github.com/manz/ff4), started in 2000 at Terminus
Traduction. The project picked up again in 2024 and grew to
cover the menus, battles and every screen of the game.
The English script comes from J2e's fan translation, reviewed
line by line against the Japanese: the lines J2e added are
gone, lines are back with their speakers, and names and terms
are the same everywhere.
This version is a beta: the game can be finished, but typos
and bugs surely remain. Please report them (see 6).

----------------------------------------------------------------
3.  ARCHIVE CONTENTS AND USAGE
----------------------------------------------------------------

The ZIP archive should contain:

ff4.bps		- The FF4 English BPS patch @VERSION@, to apply
		  to the original Japanese rom (Flips, beat or
		  some emulators). It checks the rom
		  and refuses a wrong one.

ff4.ips		- The same patch as IPS, for tools that don't
		  read BPS.

README.txt	- The file you are reading

Apply the patch ONLY to the Japanese Final Fantasy IV rom
(headerless, 1 MB):
  CRC32  CAA15E97

The patch version shows at the bottom left of the New Game
menu.

4.  WHAT WAS DONE
----------------------------------------------------------------

@VERSION@:

- Full translation: dialogue, menus, items, spells, monsters,
  places, battles.
- Script reviewed against the Japanese (J2e's additions
  removed).
- Variable-width font in dialogue, menus and battles.
- Full item, spell and monster names everywhere.
- Scrolling item lists: inventory, equipment, shops, chests,
  battle.
- Display bugs and crashes fixed.

The full changelog comes with the release on GitHub.
